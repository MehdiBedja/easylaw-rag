# EasyLaw — Algerian law assistant (Arabic · French)

Ask a legal question in Arabic or French: the answer is grounded in the Algerian *Journal officiel*, cites its
sources, links to the official PDFs and flags texts that were amended or repealed.

Code, method and evaluation: **https://github.com/MehdiBedja/easylaw-rag**

This free demo runs on 2 CPU cores and a free language-model tier: an answer takes about 20–40 s, the first visit
after a pause waits a few minutes for the service to start, and questions are limited per visitor.
*General legal information, not legal advice.*
