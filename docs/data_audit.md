## 7. Multi-page truncation

A text's page span is estimated as the pages until the next text in the same journal starts (the last text of each journal is excluded). Multi-page texts whose OCR is no longer than a typical single-page text probably only have their first page.

- Median length of single-page texts: 1,737 chars
- Multi-page texts that look first-page-only: **34.3%** of 38,970
